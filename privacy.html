Privacy Policy

AL Tools for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective 8 November 2026

Summary. AL Tools does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to us or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. It talks only to the SSH servers you configure, and everything it stores stays on your device.

Who we are

AL Tools is published by an independent developer. Contact details are at the end of this page.

What the app stores on your device

To do its job, AL Tools keeps the following information locally on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. None of it is sent to us.

Server profiles — the names, host names, ports, user names and folders you enter for your servers — are stored in the app's local preferences.

Credentials — passwords you choose to save, SSH private keys the app generates, and the fingerprints of server host keys you have accepted — are stored only in the device Keychain, protected by Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Keys never leave the device; only the public half is shown so you can install it on your server.

Downloaded files you choose to download are saved in the app's own folder (On My iPhone/iPad → AL Tools, or the Downloads folder on Mac) and can be deleted at any time from the Downloads screen or the Files app.

Workspace settings for the AI Workspace (which CLI you selected and its path, per server) are stored in local preferences.

Deleting the app removes all of the above.

Where your data goes

AL Tools opens direct, encrypted SSH connections to the servers you add. File transfers, terminal sessions and AI Workspace requests travel over those connections and nowhere else. The app does not use any intermediary or cloud relay operated by us.

To run the AI Workspace, the app uploads a small helper script to the workspace folder you selected on your server. The script starts the Codex or Claude Code command-line tool that you have installed and signed in on that server, under your own account, and streams its output back to the app. Prompts and files you attach are processed by that tool and by its provider (OpenAI for Codex, Anthropic for Claude Code) according to their terms and privacy policies, exactly as if you had typed the same prompt into the tool yourself. AL Tools does not include or provide access to any AI model and never sees your prompts outside the connection to your server.

What we do not do

We do not collect usage analytics, crash reports, identifiers, location, contacts, or any other personal data. We do not use tracking technologies and we do not show advertising. The app makes no network connections other than to the servers you configure.

Permissions the app asks for

Face ID / Touch ID is used only to unlock the app and to authorise access to credentials stored in the Keychain. Biometric data itself is handled entirely by the operating system and is never available to the app.

Local network access is requested on iOS so that you can connect to servers on your own Wi-Fi network. The app does not scan or discover devices.

Background processing is used only to finish a file transfer that is in progress when you leave the app.

Children

AL Tools is a developer tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Because the app collects nothing, we expect changes to be rare.
